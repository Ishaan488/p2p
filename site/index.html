STEP 01

Start on Windows

Extract the zip and run Install-P2P-Link.bat once. It configures the firewall and adds "Send via P2P Link" to your right-click menu.

STEP 02

Scan the QR code

Open the Android app and scan the QR code displayed on your computer to pair your devices.

STEP 03

Share from anywhere

Share directly from your phone's Gallery share sheet, or right-click any file or folder in Windows Explorer.

Installing the APK: If Android prompts on first open, tap Settings and enable Allow from this source.
Campus or office Wi-Fi: If public Wi-Fi blocks local connections, turn on your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot (mobile data can stay off) and connect your PC.
